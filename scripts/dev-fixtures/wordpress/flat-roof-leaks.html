Flat roofs leak more often than pitched roofs because water has nowhere to run off. Most leaks start small, so a yearly check saves money. [caption id="attachment_7" align="aligncenter" width="800"] Ponding water after a winter storm[/caption]

Why flat roofs leak

The most common causes are:
  • Ponding water that sits for more than 48 hours
  • Cracked flashing around vents and skylights
  • Seams that open up as the membrane ages
EPDM and TPO membranes usually last 20 to 25 years. The NRCA's flat roof guide explains how each one ages.

How to fix a flat roof leak

Call a roofer first. Don't walk on a wet membrane, and don't seal the seams with caulk from the hardware store. A patch costs $300 to $1,000; replacing a whole membrane starts around $6 per square foot.
