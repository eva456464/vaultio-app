How much does a metal roof cost?

Most homeowners in Santa Barbara pay between $14,000 and $28,000 for a metal roof on a 1,700 square foot house. The price depends on the panel type, the roof's pitch and how many layers come off first.

  • Materials: $5–$12 per square foot
  • Labor: $3–$6 per square foot
  • Tear-off of the old roof: $1–$2 per square foot

Is a metal roof worth it?

Pro tip: ask every roofer which panel gauge they quote. A 24-gauge panel costs more than 29-gauge but dents far less.

Metal roofs last 40 to 70 years, about twice as long as asphalt shingles, and reflect heat in summer. See this comparison for more.
