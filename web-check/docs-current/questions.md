# FAQ

Common questions about BabyLoveGrowth

## [Getting Started](#getting-started)

I am not an SEO expert?

Don't worry! BabyLoveGrowth is designed to support individuals without any SEO knowledge. We take care of everything from keyword research, clustering, content creation to content optimization. Connect your website once and new articles publish automatically, or download them if you prefer.

Do I need to write the articles myself?

No, the whole process is 100% automated and ensures that the content fulfills the user's intent and ranks on Google.

What kind of articles do you produce?

BabyLoveGrowth generates well-researched, citation-backed articles written in your brand's tone and voice, tailored to your target audience. We produce various article types: how-to guides, explainer guides, educational listicles, product roundups, and product comparisons, each matched to the keyword's intent (informational, commercial, or transactional).

## [Content Generation](#content-generation)

How can you generate such high quality content?

Our content generation process is fully automatic: AI models use deep research and fact-based sources to craft high-quality, SEO-optimized articles, with no person writing them. We initiate this process by conducting an in-depth analysis of your webpage to identify topics that resonate with your service or product offerings and target audience. Then, we employ keyword research and clustering techniques to come up with the most relevant and high-potential informational keywords. By examining the existing top-ranking content on Google for these keywords, we develop a comprehensive outline that encompasses all crucial aspects and subtopics. This outline serves as the foundation for generating well-structured articles. Additionally, we ensure the content is optimized for search engines, engaging, and informative, incorporating elements such as relevant images, internal links, FAQ sections, and more.

Are internal links supported?

Yes, internal links are supported. Each article generated by BabyLoveGrowth includes up to 10 internal links to other relevant articles on your website. Additionally, we also include a "recommended article" section at the end of each article, suggesting further reading materials to your audience.

In which format are the articles generated?

All articles are generated in Markdown format and published directly on your website through one of many CMS integrations (Wordpress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, API,..). You can also download them to your computer.

Why do my articles mention competitors or other products?

This is a critical SEO strategy, not a mistake. It's a special article type 'product listicle comparison'. Google and AI search engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) prioritize comprehensive, unbiased comparisons over promotional content that only mentions one product. If you search "best CRM software" on Google right now, every top result compares multiple products (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.) This is what ranks, what users expect when they search, and what BabyLoveGrowth creates.

When you objectively compare options, you become an authoritative source instead of a salesperson. This builds trust, increases engagement, and matches search intent. People searching for solutions want to see their options, not just read an advertisement.

You can also disable this article type in **Settings → Writing → Mention Competitors When Relevant**.

## [Languages](#languages)

Can I write articles in multiple languages?

Yes. Add languages with **Add language** in the website menu at the top of the dashboard to expand reach. Articles can be generated and published per locale.

Are the daily articles in different languages purely translated?

No, the daily articles are not purely translated. For each language, we perform extensive keyword research to ensure we tailor the content to use localized keywords and phrases (with ranking potential).

Which languages are supported?

We support content generation in 32 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Polish, Chinese, and Turkish.

## [Publishing & Display](#publishing--display)

Why doesn't my article look good on my site?

Formatting, layout, and mobile-friendliness on your site depend on your website's theme and CSS, not BabyLoveGrowth. We deliver clean, semantic content (headings, paragraphs, lists, etc.); your theme controls how it looks (fonts, spacing, responsiveness). If an article doesn't look the way you expect, adjust your theme or add custom CSS for your blog/post templates. See [Article Quality](/docs/dashboard/article-quality) for more.

Webflow: Blog content not displaying in the Editor?

This is a known issue from the Webflow team. Blog content may not display properly in the Webflow Editor (e.g. visible code, broken layout, Rich Text field issues). You can refer to [this confirmation from Webflow](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EHzmC5ZgHaMCA-uN0KLFzX0lYWROMyXe/view?usp=sharing). Content publishes successfully and will be visible on your live website. See our [Webflow integration guide](/docs/integrations/webflow) for more details.

## [Ranking & Results](#ranking--results)

Will Google penalize AI written content?

No, Google penalizes low quality content, no matter if it's AI or human written. Their official take on AI content is available here: [Google Search and AI Content](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content).

Can AI content even rank on Google?

Yes, absolutely. If executed correctly. We've proven multiple times that quality AI content can rank well on Google. When AI has a sufficient context window to understand user intent and access to high-quality, credible information about the topic, it produces excellent content. Proper citation prevents hallucinations, and using the best LLM models available ensures quality. We handle all of this for you.

My articles aren't ranking on Google yet. Why?

SEO takes time, and this is completely normal. New articles are usually indexed within a few days to 4 weeks; submitting your sitemap in Google Search Console can speed this up. You may see first movement in impressions and rankings around weeks 4-8.

ChatGPT and other AI search engines can start citing new content within a few weeks after it's indexed, but there's no fixed timeline. While waiting, keep publishing consistently and don't judge results too early.

How long does it typically take to see results?

SEO usually takes 4-6 months to deliver meaningful results. For new websites, or sites with little existing optimization, it can take up to 12 months. The 3-day trial is too short to judge SEO results.

## [Backlinks](#backlinks)

How does the backlink exchange work?

Become part of a backlink exchange network of 5,000+ sites where we automatically exchange backlinks with other niche-relevant customers by naturally inserting them within the daily articles.

Which niches are allowed for backlink exchange?

To maintain the quality and integrity of our backlink network, we carefully curate participating websites. We welcome most legitimate business niches. We exclude sites that mainly sell illegal drugs, pharmaceuticals or supplements, or crypto. CBD, tobacco and vaping, adult, and gambling sites are accepted where legal: CBD, adult, and gambling sites only exchange backlinks with sites in the same category, while tobacco and vaping sites join the normal exchange.

## [Billing & Subscription](#billing--subscription)

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime. Go to **Settings → Subscription** and click **Cancel Subscription**. After confirming, your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period, and no further charges occur. Content stays yours and you can continue to use it as you see fit.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 3-day free trial period to ensure our service meets your needs. During this time, you can fully evaluate the quality and suitability of our content generation. After the trial ends we don't offer refunds, except under our 90-day money-back guarantee or when our team confirms a bug or that the product was not as advertised.

Do you offer volume discounts for multiple websites?

Yes. Each website has its own subscription, and each new website gets a discount on its plan at checkout, based on how many active websites you already have:

- **2-4 websites:** 10% off the new website's plan
- **5-8 websites:** 12% off the new website's plan
- **9-14 websites:** 15% off the new website's plan
- **15+ websites:** 20% off the new website's plan

So your second website is full price, and the discount starts with your third. It's applied automatically.
