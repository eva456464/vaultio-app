# Bolt.dev

Use Blog Hosting to add a blog to your Bolt.dev app with one DNS record.

For Bolt.dev apps, use our [Blog Hosting](/docs/integrations/blog-hosting) feature - just add one DNS record and we'll host your blog at `blog.yoursite.com`.

[](/docs/integrations/blog-hosting)

### View Blog Hosting Setup Guide

When you use Blog Hosting (as above), BabyLoveGrowth controls styling and mobile responsiveness. You can change your logo in the Blog Hosting wizard (**Settings → Publishing**) and your color in **Settings → Branding**. If you self-host your blog on your own site instead, appearance depends on your theme and CSS. [Learn more →](/docs/integrations/blog-hosting#customization-options)
