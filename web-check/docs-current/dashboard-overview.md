# Overview

Initial page you see when you log in to BabyLovegrowth.

When you land on the 'Overview' dashboard, you'll see what our agents are doing for your website, what we learned about your business, and, once your website is connected, the results they're bringing in.

## [What your agents did today](#what-your-agents-did-today)

The **Agent activities** card lists everything our agents did for your website on the selected day, with the total number of activities at the top. Examples include writing and publishing articles, getting you new backlinks, checking whether AI recommends you, scanning your site for technical issues, and finding Reddit threads and Quora questions to join.

- **Day stepper:** use the arrows to move to previous days and see what was done, or to upcoming days to see what's planned.
- **Featured article:** the day's article, with its target keyword, monthly search volume, and keyword difficulty (Easy, Medium, or Hard). Expand **Why this topic?** to see why we picked it.
- **Planned actions:** what your agents will do over the next few days.

Next to it you'll see either the **Get started** checklist or your new milestones:

- **Get started:** connect your website (required), plus optional steps such as connecting Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and Instagram, watching the walkthrough video, and reading the docs.
- **Milestones:** when you unlock a new achievement, it takes the checklist's place until you dismiss it.

## [Your website's achievements](#your-websites-achievements)

Once your website is connected, this card shows your organic wins: milestones your articles have reached on Google and in AI search, such as clicks, impressions, indexed articles, and top rankings. Connect Google Search Console to track organic milestones and Google Analytics 4 to track AI search milestones. Click **View proof** on any achievement to see the data behind it.

## [Here's what we learned about your brand](#heres-what-we-learned-about-your-brand)

A summary of what we know about your business: what you do, what you offer, your brand colors, who you serve, where you sell, and your competitors. We use this to plan and write every article for you. Click **Looks good** to confirm it, or **Not quite right, update** to correct it.

## [Where your traffic comes from](#where-your-traffic-comes-from)

Once your website is connected, this card shows which channels bring visitors to your site. Connect Google Analytics and Search Console to see the data. Open [Analytics](/docs/dashboard/analytics) for the full report.

## [Success story](#success-story)

A BabyLoveGrowth customer in your industry that started at a similar traffic level, with their growth curve over time and where you are on it today.

## [You're in good hands](#youre-in-good-hands)

Our Google rating and the money-back guarantee, with a link to the eligibility terms.
