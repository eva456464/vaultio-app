# WordPress.com

Connect a WordPress.com site in one click and publish through WordPress.com's own API, no plugin required.

Publish your BabyLoveGrowth articles straight to a site hosted on **WordPress.com**. You connect once by approving BabyLoveGrowth on WordPress.com. There is no plugin to install, no application password to create, and no credentials to copy.

Self-hosted WordPress?

This integration is only for sites hosted on WordPress.com. If you run WordPress on your own server, use the [WordPress integration](/docs/integrations/wordpress) instead.

How articles look on your site

Formatting and mobile-friendliness depend on your website's theme and CSS. We deliver semantic content; your theme controls the styling. [Learn more](/docs/dashboard/article-quality)

## [How to connect](#how-to-connect)

1.  Open **Settings → Publishing** and find **WordPress.com** under **Connect Your Website**.
2.  Click **Connect** on the card, then **Connect** in the dialog. You are taken to WordPress.com.
3.  Sign in if needed, pick the site you want to publish to, and approve access.
4.  WordPress.com sends you back to the dashboard and the card shows **Connected**.

If you decline on the WordPress.com screen, nothing is connected and you can simply start again.

## [Publish settings](#publish-settings)

Click **Manage** on the WordPress.com card to change how articles arrive:

- **Published immediately**: each new article goes live as soon as it is generated.
- **Draft**: articles are created as drafts for you to review and publish yourself.

## [What happens on publish](#what-happens-on-publish)

- The article is posted through WordPress.com's API, so your site's firewall or security plugins are never in the way.
- Images are uploaded into your site's media library.
- If a post with the same slug already exists, it is updated instead of duplicated.

## [Article language](#article-language)

WordPress.com can only tag a post's language when the site runs **WPML** or **Polylang**, and those plugins need a **Business plan**. Without one of them, posts are published without a specific language set.

This only matters if your BabyLoveGrowth website publishes in a language other than English. The dashboard shows a reminder when it does.

## [Reconnecting](#reconnecting)

If you remove BabyLoveGrowth's access on WordPress.com, the card shows **Action needed** and publishing pauses until you connect again. Click **Connect** and approve access once more; your publish settings are kept.

## [Disconnecting](#disconnecting)

Click **Manage**, then **Disconnect**. Articles stop being published to WordPress.com, and anything already published stays on your site.
