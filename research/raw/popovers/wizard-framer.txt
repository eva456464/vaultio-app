Step 5 of 6

Build your blog pages in Framer
Your articles are in Framer. Now create pages to display them:
1
In Framer, open the Pages panel (left sidebar), click the +, and choose New CMS Page.
2
Select the collection you named earlier (e.g. "Blog"), then choose the Index template — this becomes your blog listing page that shows all articles.
3
Add another page: + → New CMS Page → the same collection → choose the Detail template (Framer usually names it /blog/:slug). This is the individual article page.
4
On both pages, bind the elements to your CMS fields — title, cover image, and a Formatted Text element for the content — then style and publish your Framer site.
