Step 3 of 5

Add the RSS feed to Duda
Add Duda's built-in RSS Feed widget to the page where you want your articles, then paste your feed URL.
Do this in the Duda editor. Open your site from your Duda dashboard:
Open your Duda sites
1
Open your site in the Duda Website Editor.
2
In the left sidebar, click Add → Elements, search “RSS Feed”, and drag the widget onto the page where you want your articles.
3
The RSS Feed Content panel usually opens automatically when you drop the widget. If it doesn't, right-click the widget and select Edit Content (in Flex mode and Editor 2.0, use the widget's floating Edit Content button).
4
Paste the RSS feed URL you copied into the field, adjust the display settings (number of posts, layout), then click Publish.
The RSS Feed widget displays your articles on this page, it doesn't add them to Duda's native Blog. Want real blog posts instead? The next step imports the same feed into your Duda Blog.

Step 4 of 5

Import your articles into Duda's Blog
Duda's native Blog can import the same feed, turning your articles into real blog posts on your site.
Do this in the Duda editor, on the site where you want the posts:
Open your Duda sites
1
Open your site in the Duda Website Editor.
2
In the left sidebar, click Blog, then open the Import tab.
3
Paste the RSS feed URL you copied into the Enter RSS feed URL field.
4
Click Import Posts, then Publish your site so the posts go live.
