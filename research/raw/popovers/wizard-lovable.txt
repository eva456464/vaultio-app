Step 4 of 6

Paste your API key when Lovable asks
Lovable opens a secure secret field for your key. Paste it there.
Never paste your API key into the normal chat box. Chat messages are saved in your project history. Only use the secure secret field Lovable opens for you.
1
Wait for Lovable to ask for BABYLOVEGROWTH_API_KEY.
2
Paste the key from the previous step into the secure field it opens.
3
Confirm, and Lovable continues building and starts importing your articles.

Step 5 of 6

Check your blog, then publish
Lovable builds the pages and pulls in your articles. Review it, then push it live.
1
When Lovable finishes, open /blog in the preview to check it.
2
Ask Lovable in plain language for any design changes you want.
3
Click Publish in Lovable to push the blog live.
The first import takes a few minutes. Our API is rate limited, so articles arrive in batches of 20 and the import keeps running in the background on its own. If your blog looks short at first, wait a few minutes and refresh.
