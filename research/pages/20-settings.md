# 20–39 · Settings — `/:locale/dashboard/settings/<tab>`

Every tab has a raw dossier `pages/<NN>-settings-<tab>.auto.md` (full visible text, every form control with label/value, buttons, API shapes) and screenshots `screenshots/<NN>-settings-<tab>-*.png`. Docs: `docs/dashboard-settings.md` (21K chars — very detailed), `docs/dashboard-product-catalog.md`, `docs/whitelabel.md`.

**Layout:** settings replace the main sidebar with a settings nav (`← Back to dashboard`), groups:
- ACCOUNT: `Profile` (account), `Members` (team-members), `Subscription` (billing)
- WHITELABEL: `Branding and domains` (white-label) — agency orgs only
- BUSINESS & BRAND: `Business details`, `Branding`, `Competitors`, `Product Catalog`, `Saved pages`
- ARTICLES: `Writing`, `Visuals`, `Schedule`, `Improvements` (Beta)
- INTEGRATIONS: `Publishing`, `Analytics`
- SOCIALS: `Reddit`, `Quora` (BLG also `Instagram & Facebook` (meta); `Google Business` (gbp, redirects — "Soon"); `Refer and earn` (referrals))
Content column ~770px centered on `#FAFAF8`; each section is a white card with title + grey description; changes auto-save or have Save buttons per card (writes → `PUT /organization/update-org-website` etc.).
