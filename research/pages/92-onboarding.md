## Customer steps (exact copy)
1. **Step 1 of 5 — `What's your website?`** (`Let's begin`) — `Enter your site and we'll build an SEO & AI search strategy around your business and market.` Input placeholder `Insert your website (example.com)`. Errors: invalid URL; `Please enter your business website URL. Social media profiles, Google My Business, and other non-website URLs are not allowed.`; `site-builder URLs (.webflow.io, .lovable.app, etc.) aren't supported`; `We couldn't find that domain…`; browser check (Cloudflare Turnstile); `Onboarding already completed for this website.` Agency variant: `Add a client` / `Enter your client's website URL.`; duplicate URL warning. API: `POST /organization/create-org-website` (or `/guest-onboarding/start`), `check-website-dns`, `detect-platform`; screenshot taken.
2. **Step 2 of 5 — `Target market & language`** — `Choose the market you want to rank in and the language your content is written in.` Fields `Target market` (country/region or Global; tooltip), `Primary language` (searchable), `Potential audience` figure. Loading `Detecting your market and language` / `Analyzing your website — this only takes a few seconds.` Unsupported-language notice.
3. **Step 3 of 5 — `Describe Your Business`** — loading `We are analyzing your business` (`approx 15 seconds`) → AI-filled description + target audiences (min 2: `At least 2 target audiences are required` / `Great, that's enough to build your content strategy`). Unreachable site: `We couldn't reach {url}` + `Restart onboarding`. API: `generate-description-from-url` / `business-info`. Preview: `We turn your input into a content strategy`.
4. **Step 4 of 5 — `Select your competitors` (Optional)** — `We are searching for your competitors` → `We found your main competitors` / `These websites compete with you for relevant search traffic`; preselected list, add/remove. API: `get-organic-competitors`.
5. **Step 5 of 5 — `Let us know your brand`** — `Brand Color`, `Secondary Color`, `Brand Description` (AI-generated aesthetic, editable), `Example Article URL (optional)` (brand voice), `Article cover image` style picker (Realistic with title / Realistic / …). API: `detect-brand-colors`. Preview: `Your brand color in action` — Branded images, Custom charts, Consistent visuals.
6. **Connect Google Search Console** (optional) — `Connecting helps us understand what kind of unique content to create. We get view-only access…`; `Continue` / `Continue without connecting`.
7. **Survey** — `How did you hear about BabyLoveGrowth?` options incl. Friend (email), LinkedIn creator (name), Other (specify).
8. **Select plan** — `Start free. See your strategy live.` `Start my {days}-day free trial`, `Grow Plan`, `Best value`, `Outrank {domain} and others`, `Start Your 3-Day Free Trial`, `Cancel anytime. No questions asked.` → Stripe Checkout (`create-customer-checkout`) or `Reactivate Subscription`.
After payment: `POST /onboarding/start` → background pipeline (brand profile, 10 competitors, ~1.5K keywords, 10 AI prompts, 31-article first-month plan, backlink exchange enrollment, first DR/site-health/AI checks) → Overview shows these as day-1 activities.

## Agency setup steps
1. `What's your agency website?` — `We use it to prefill your brand name, app domain and logo. We never track or write content for it.` (`youragency.com`).
2. `How should your clients' dashboard look?` — choose **White label BabyLoveGrowth** (`Your clients log in to their own dashboard on your domain, with your logo and colors. They never see BabyLoveGrowth.`) vs normal (`Your agency name, logo and colors appear on your clients' reports. Your dashboard uses BabyLoveGrowth branding.`); app domain (suggest `app.youragency.com`), sending email address; preview `This is what your clients will see` (fake browser with address bar, nav, `Welcome`, `Everything is running`, `Today's article`, achievements `We've built $157.2K of value for you`, invite email mock `Hey there! … Accept Invitation`).
3. `Connect your domain (optional)` — DNS records → `Start onboarding clients`.
4. `Invite your team` — `Teammate's email` `name@youragency.com`.
5. `Book a call with your account manager` — 15-minute call → `Go to my dashboard`.
