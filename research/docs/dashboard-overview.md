# Overview

Initial page you see when you log in to BabyLovegrowth.

When you land on the 'Overview' dashboard, you'll see a snapshot of every channel BabyLovegrowth is working on for you: content, backlinks, search and LLM visibility, social presence on Reddit and Quora, and everything our agents have completed for your website.

## [Content Plan](#content-plan)

The **Today's article** card shows the article we're creating for you today.

- **Title and URL:** the article's headline, its cover image, and the address where it will live on your blog.
- **Search volume:** how many people search this keyword on Google every month.
- **Keyword difficulty:** how hard it is to reach the first page of Google for this search, from 0 (easy) to 100 (very hard).
- **Article type:** the kind of article we write (for example, an explainer), chosen to match what people searching this actually want.
- **Why this topic?**, **Why this structure?** and **Article characteristics:** expand these to see why we picked the keyword and how the article is built.

![Today's article card showing the article title, URL, search volume, keyword difficulty, article type, and the Why this topic, Why this structure, and Article characteristics sections](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/overview-content-plan.ac855ff4.png)

## [Backlinks](#backlinks)

- **Backlinks Received:** trend of backlinks acquired through BabyLovegrowth over the last several months, with a forecasted dotted line for the months ahead.
- **Domain Rating:** your current domain authority score, tracked over time so you can see how your link profile is compounding.

![Backlinks card showing Backlinks Received chart and Domain Rating](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/overview-backlinks.9e24e1d3.png)

## [Google and LLM analytics](#google-and-llm-analytics)

**Website traffic** shows where your visitors come from: Google search and AI assistants.

- **AI search traffic:** how many people come to your website from Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
- **Google traffic:** **Clicks** (people who clicked through from Google to your site), **Impressions** (how many times your site showed up in Google results), and **Average position** (where your pages show up on average; lower is better, 1 is the top result). The green arrow under clicks and impressions shows how much they've grown.

**LLM visibility score** shows how often AI assistants name your brand.

- **Visibility score:** out of all the questions you track, how often AI assistants named your brand.
- **Monitored prompts:** how many questions we ask AI assistants to check whether your brand comes up.

Click the arrow on any card to open the full report: [Analytics](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/dashboard/analytics) for your traffic, or AI Visibility for your score.

![Website traffic card with AI search traffic and Google traffic, next to the LLM visibility score card](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/overview-google-llm.476acb66.png)

## [Social presence](#social-presence)

- **Pending Opportunities:** Reddit and Quora posts where your brand can be mentioned, with a progress bar showing how many have already been completed.
- **Potential Reach:** the total audience size across pending opportunities, plus the equivalent ad spend it would take to buy that visibility, so you can see the value of organic Reddit and Quora reach at a glance.

![Reddit and Quora card showing pending opportunities and potential reach](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/overview-social.731f094b.png)

## [Agent activity](#agent-activity)

**Agent activity** lists the latest work completed for your website, newest first, with the total number of actions next to the title. Each row shows the date and what was done, for example:

- **Wrote article** or **Published article**, with the article title
- **New backlink from** another website
- **Ran Reddit monitoring scan** and **Ran Quora monitoring scan**
- AI visibility and site health scans

Switch between **List** and **Calendar** in the top-right corner. The calendar shows everything completed for your website in the last year, day by day.

![Agent activity card listing recent articles, Reddit and Quora scans, and new backlinks, with List and Calendar views](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/overview-agent.5880f4d3.png)
