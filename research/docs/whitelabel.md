# White-Label

Agencies can whitelabel the whole babylovegrowth solution. You can read more about it below.

<https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/en/whitelabel>

What is the White Label Solution and how much does it cost?

Our White Label Solution allows you to offer our platform under your own brand. Your clients will see your logo, domain, and brand colors throughout the entire dashboard experience. Setup is free, and you pay $99 per website per month with bulk discounts available for additional websites.

How long does the white label setup take?

You set it up yourself in a few minutes. We show you the DNS records to add, and your domain connects as soon as they resolve, usually within minutes but up to 48 hours with some providers.

What information do I need to provide for white label setup?

To set up your white label solution, you'll need to provide:

- Your logo in SVG or PNG format (including both icon and full logo versions)
- The subdomain where you want to host the application (e.g., app.yourdomain.io)
- Primary and secondary brand colors in hex codes (e.g., #8B5CF6)
- The email address you want to use for sending emails to clients
- Your billing address for invoicing
- DNS access to configure the required records we'll provide

What DNS configuration is required?

During setup, we show you the specific DNS records that need to be added to your domain settings. This includes subdomain configuration and email sending domain setup. You'll need to add these values to your DNS provider. We can also offer a dedicated sending subdomain option to help isolate your sending domain reputation and avoid interference with your current email setup.

Is the backend completely white labeled?

Yes, the backend is fully white labeled with your brand colors, logo, and domain. Your clients will see your branding everywhere - from login screens to the full dashboard interface, including articles, content plan, received and given backlinks, GEO audit, and GSC analytics. The WordPress plugin and Shopify integration are also available in white label versions. There will be no visible indication that BabyLoveGrowth powers the solution.

What's the difference between White Label and Direct Onboarding?

You have two options:

**White Label Solution** (free setup + $99/website/month): Clients get full dashboard access under your brand. They can view content plan, backlinks, GEO audit, and analytics in your branded interface. Bulk discounts apply.

**Grow plan** ($99/website/month): You manage everything through our platform and configure CMS integrations. Clients just see published articles on their website without needing dashboard access.

Choose White Label if you want clients to have dashboard access under your brand. Choose Grow if you prefer to manage everything yourself and clients don't need backend access.

How does authentication work with white label?

Currently, white label solutions support email/password authentication only. Google Auth and other OAuth providers are not supported for white label domains. If your account was originally set up with Google Auth, you'll need to reset your password to use email authentication on your white label domain. We recommend setting up accounts with email/password authentication from the start.

How do the WordPress and Shopify integrations work?

**WordPress Plugin**: We provide a white labeled WordPress plugin as a .zip file that you can share with your clients. Simply import it in the WordPress plugin section and activate it. The plugin will be branded with your company name and logo. A white label plugin for the WordPress store is also being prepared.

**Shopify Integration**: The Shopify integration is fully white labeled and includes updated instructions for the changes Shopify made to their API/App features in January 2026. We provide video tutorials and documentation to guide you through the integration process.

Can I add multiple websites and customize branding after setup?

Yes, you can add unlimited websites within your existing organization at $99 per website with bulk discounts. You can change your logo and brand colors at any time in Settings under Agency, in Branding and domains.

How do I manage client accounts and which organization should I connect?

You onboard clients through our platform just as you would normally. When setting up white label, you'll need to specify which user organization (email account) you want connected to your white label domain. All websites and clients under that organization will be accessible through your branded dashboard. You can create client accounts with yourself as the owner and add clients as secondary members, maintaining full control over all subscriptions and billing.

What features are included in the white label dashboard?

The white label dashboard includes all platform features:

- 30 articles per month automatically generated and published
- Quality backlinks built automatically via Backlink Exchange
- Content backed by real-time research
- Internal and external linking
- Integrates with WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Wix, API
- Infographics and YouTube videos integrated into articles
- Latest statistics and expert insights
- Technical GEO Audit
- GSC Analytics
- Complete dashboard on your domain with your branding

The referral UI elements are only displayed for admin accounts by default and can be removed from client-facing interfaces if desired.

What support do I receive as a white label partner?

Since the platform is white labeled under your brand, you maintain the client relationship. However, we provide dedicated partner support to help you resolve any technical issues. You'll be assigned a team member who will guide you through the setup process and be available for any questions. You can also reach out via email or schedule calls with our team as needed.
