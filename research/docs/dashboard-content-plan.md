# Blog Content

View, modify and manage your content plan

On this screen, you’ll find a calendar view of all your planned content topics, along with important details such as keyword difficulty (KD) and search volume for each topic.

**Keyword difficulty (KD)** is a score between 0-100 and measures how challenging it is to rank for a keyword in search engine results. The higher the score, the more competitive the keyword.  
**Search volume** indicates how many times people search for that keyword per month, helping you prioritize topics with the greatest traffic potential.

You can also review previously generated articles, switch out planned topics, and give specific instructions to guide article creation.

![Content Plan calendar showing scheduled and published articles](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/content-plan-1.3487d19f.png)

## [Delete Topic](#delete-topic)
