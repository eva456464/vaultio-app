# Settings

Configure your account, business and brand, article settings, integrations, and socials.

The settings sidebar is organized into five groups. Each group collects the pages that belong to one job:

- **Account** is your profile, workspace members, subscription, and referrals.
- **Business & brand** is who you are: business details, branding, competitors, and product catalog.
- **Article settings** is how articles get made: writing, visuals, and schedule.
- **Integrations** is where content goes and how it is measured: publishing and analytics.
- **Socials** is how we find opportunities to reply on Reddit and Quora.

## [Account](#account)

### [Profile](#profile)

Manage your personal user information, preferences, and security.

- **Name:** Update your display name shown across the platform.
- **Email:** Your login email address. Contact support to change this.
- **Change Password:** Update your account password for security.
- **Dashboard Language:** Select your preferred interface language.
- **Product Updates:** Toggle notifications about new features and updates.
- **Danger zone:** Delete your account. Deletion is scheduled 14 days in advance. During that time you can restore your account with one click. After 14 days the deletion becomes permanent.

![Profile settings showing name, email, language, and notification preferences](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/settings-account-specific.ca8af6af.png)

### [Members](#members)

Invite and manage team members who can access your workspace.

- **Workspace name:** The name shown across your team workspace.
- **Member List:** View all team members with their name, email, role, and status.
- **Roles:** Assign Admin or Member roles. Admins have full access while Members have limited permissions.
- **Status:** See who is Active or has a Pending invitation.
- **Invite Members:** Add new team members by email. They will receive an invitation to join.
- **Remove Members:** Remove team members who no longer need access.

![Members settings showing the team list with roles and status](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/settings-team.49962a96.png)

### [Subscription](#subscription)

View and manage your subscription, billing information, and payment history.

- **Current Plan:** See your active subscription plan.
- **Next Billing Date:** Know when your next payment is due.
- **Status:** Check if your subscription is active, paused, or cancelled.
- **Manage Subscription:** Upgrade, downgrade, or modify your plan.
- **Cancel Subscription:** Cancel your subscription if needed.
- **Payment History:** View all past invoices with dates, amounts, and status. Download invoice PDFs for your records.

![Subscription settings showing plan details and payment history](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/settings-billing.0f3a4e8d.png)

### [Refer and earn](#refer-and-earn)

Invite friends and colleagues. Every friend who becomes a paying customer earns you a cash reward.

- **Your referral link:** Share it anywhere; sign-ups are attributed to you.
- **Your progress:** Track how many referred friends have converted.
- **Reward tiers:** See the payout for each successful referral.
- **Your referred friends:** A list of everyone who joined through your link.

![Refer a friend card with a Share & earn button](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/settings-refer-a-friend.246caaee.png)

## [Business & brand](#business--brand)

### [Business details](#business-details)

What your business does and who it serves. This is the context every article is written from.

#### [About the business](#about-the-business)

- **Business description:** Describe your business, products, and services. Click **Generate with AI** to create a description automatically from your website.
- **Business name:** How your brand is referenced in generated articles and Reddit comments.
- **Industry:** Used to match you with relevant backlink partners. If it can't be changed and it's wrong, contact support.

![About the business card showing the business description, Generate with AI button, business name, and industry](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-business-1.0c8c69a6.png)

#### [Positioning and services](#positioning-and-services)

- **Positioning:** What sets you apart from competitors, in up to 500 characters.
- **Services and prices:** Everything your business sells, as your own website states it. Articles use this list whenever they mention what you offer or what it costs, so a wrong row here becomes a wrong claim about your business. We fill it in from your website and leave a price out whenever the page does not clearly publish one.

Each row is one offer:

- **Type:** a **Service** is work you perform, a **Plan** is a subscription tier, a **Product** is a line you make or brand, a **Fee** is a charge for using you, and a **Category** groups goods you resell. Categories have no price of their own.
- **Name:** the offer's name, exactly as your website calls it.
- **Price:** the published figure, digits only. Use a dash for a range (like 300-500), or leave it empty if you don't publish a price.
- **Currency:** such as EUR or USD, or % for a commission.
- **Billing:** what one price buys: One-off, a billing cycle (Hourly to Yearly), or **Price on request** when you don't publish a price.

Click **Add offer** to add a row, up to 20 offers.

![Positioning and services card showing the positioning text and a services and prices table with plans and categories](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-business-2.e8116448.png)

#### [About your customers](#about-your-customers)

- **Target audience:** Who your customers are, added as tags (e.g. "lawyers in Florida"). At least 2 target audiences are required.
- **Target location:** Choose the country your content should target, or leave it global. You can narrow to specific regions or cities within the country.

#### [Your website setup](#your-website-setup)

- **Blog path URL:** Your blog's home page URL (like `https://yoursite.com/blog`), so we can verify your published articles are live online.
- **Sitemap URL:** We analyze your sitemap to find internal linking opportunities. Click **View detected links** to see the pages we found.

![About your customers card with target audience tags and target location, and Your website setup card with blog path URL and sitemap URL](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-business-3.0f35ee24.png)

### [Branding](#branding)

Manage your brand identity: logo, colors, and writing voice.

- **Logo:** Upload your brand logo to automatically include it in all future article visuals.
- **Colors:** Set your primary and secondary brand colors, used in generated images and infographics.
- **Voice:** Provide a URL to an existing blog post that represents your preferred writing style and tone.

![Branding settings showing logo upload, primary and secondary colors, and a writing style example URL](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/settings-branding.8625da3e.png)

### [Competitors](#competitors)

Add competitor websites to monitor and inform your content strategy.

- **Monitor Competitors:** Enter competitor domain names to track their content.
- **Content Intelligence:** We analyze competitor content to help you identify gaps and opportunities.
- **Add or Remove:** Easily add new competitors or remove ones no longer relevant.

Competitor data helps the AI understand your market and generate content that differentiates you from others in your niche.

![Competitors settings showing monitored competitor domains](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/settings-competitors.2e1b45c6.png)

### [Product Catalog](#product-catalog)

Products from your store that articles can feature and link to. Connect your store to import your products automatically, or add them manually. We then feature them in your articles with relevant links.

There are four ways to add products:

- **Shopify:** Connect your Shopify store and import products automatically. See the [Shopify import guide](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/integrations/shopify-product-catalog).
- **WooCommerce:** Connect your WooCommerce store and import products automatically. See the [WooCommerce import guide](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/integrations/woocommerce-product-catalog).
- **Add manually:** Add products one by one, upload a file, or import a Google or Facebook feed.
- **XML Feed (URL):** Import products from an existing XML feed URL.

See the [Product Catalog guide](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/dashboard/product-catalog) for details.

![Product catalog settings showing the Shopify, WooCommerce, Add manually, and XML Feed (URL) options for adding products](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-setting-PC.0ac6a6a1.png)

## [Article Settings](#article-settings)

### [Writing](#writing)

Language, length, tone and instructions for every article.

#### [Writing instructions](#writing-instructions)

Anything every future article should do or avoid: words, claims, topics, audience, tone, facts about your business. This is optional.

![Writing instructions card with the See example rules button and a field to add a rule](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-writing-1.1cd46d61.png)

#### [Language & length](#language--length)

How the writing sounds and how long it runs.

- **English Type:** Choose American, British, Australian/New Zealand, Canadian, or South African spelling and conventions.
- **Citation Region:** Choose which region to pull citation sources from, from Worldwide down to a single country, or turn citations off entirely.
- **Article Length:** Pick a target word count, or leave it on **Dynamic** to let the length adapt to what the search intent calls for. The fixed options are roughly 1,200 to 1,600, 1,700 to 2,300, and 2,400 to 2,800 words.

![Language and length settings showing English type, citation region, and article length](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-writing-2.bdfd1774.png)

#### [What every article includes](#what-every-article-includes)

Toggle the structural elements added to each article:

- **Author Perspective:** Adds a section with the author's own take on the subject.
- **Table of Contents (TOC):** Adds a linked table of contents after the intro, listing the article's main sections.
- **Recommended Reads:** Adds a section linking to related articles from your site.
- **Sources section:** Adds a list of the external sources the article cites, placed just before the FAQ. Turn it off to keep the inline links but drop the list.
- **Mention competitors when relevant:** Lets us name other brands where it makes comparison and listicle content more complete and credible for Google and AI search. Turn it off if you would rather we never mention competitors.
- **Schema Markup (JSON-LD):** Adds Article and FAQ structured data when relevant, so Google can understand your content and show rich results.
- **Summary:** Adds a recap right after the introduction, before the table of contents, for readers who skim. Pick its style:
  - **TL;DR:** 3-5 bullet points in a highlighted box.
  - **Key takeaways:** a one-sentence claim and a Point / Details table.

![What every article includes toggles, with the TL;DR and Key takeaways summary styles](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-writing-3.d27f534f.png)

Calls to action (link, contact and map cards, and the promo paragraph) are now set in [Visuals](#calls-to-action-ctas).

#### [Internal Linking](#internal-linking)

- **Enable internal links in articles:** Automatically link to your other content from within articles.
- **Add 'www' prefix to internal links:** Append www to generated internal links if your site uses it.
- **Add '/' trailing slash to internal links:** Append a trailing slash to generated internal links if your URLs use one.

Match these two toggles to how your site actually serves URLs, otherwise internal links can land on a redirect instead of the page itself.

![Internal linking settings showing toggles for internal links, www prefix, and trailing slash](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-writing-4.610603d9.png)

#### [Article disclaimer](#article-disclaimer)

Optional text appended word-for-word to the end of every article, after the last section. Leave it blank to add nothing.

![Article disclaimer settings showing the disclaimer text field](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-writing-5.39f862fd.png)

### [Visuals](#visuals)

How the article looks and where you want to send readers to your website. Each card shows where its element appears in the article (for example, **Top of the article** or **Inside the article body**). Colors and style adapt to your brand; click **Edit branding** to change them in [Branding](#branding).

#### [Cover image style](#cover-image-style)

Shown at the top of the article. Pick the style that best fits your articles; you can always change it later. Click the eye icon on any style to see examples.

- **Realistic with title:** A lifelike photo of what the article is about, with your title designed into the image like a magazine cover.
- **3D illustration:** Stylized 3D illustration with depth and dimension.
- **Sketch with title:** Hand-drawn sketch look, like pencil or ink on paper. Your title sits in the center in your brand color.
- **Sketch:** Hand-drawn sketch look, like pencil or ink on paper. No title included.
- **Watercolor with title:** Soft, painted watercolor look. Your title sits in the center in your brand color.
- **Realistic:** A lifelike photo of what the article is about. No title included.

![Cover image style options: Realistic with title, 3D illustration, Sketch with title, Sketch, Watercolor with title, and Realistic](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-visual-1.a12a5a46.png)

#### [Body image style](#body-image-style)

The images inside the article body.

- **Realistic:** Natural, lifelike photos that illustrate the article.
- **Sketch:** Hand-drawn sketches that explain the topic visually.
- **How your brand should look in images:** A short description of your visual mood and style (for example, "Warm, minimal, natural light, earthy tones; real people, no stock-photo feel"). It guides every generated image and the cover. Colors and logo live under [Branding](#branding).

![Body image style options Realistic and Sketch, with the field describing how your brand should look in images](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-visual-2.313a635d.png)

#### [Infographic style](#infographic-style)

When an article has a section worth charting, such as steps, figures or a comparison, it gets one infographic made from that section, inside the article body. Choose the look:

- **Editorial:** A magazine-style sheet with illustrations drawn for the article's content.
- **Classic:** A typeset layout of cards and charts under a photo or sketch header.

![Infographic style options Editorial and Classic, each showing an example chart](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-visual-3.ccf8c593.png)

#### [Calls to action (CTAs)](#calls-to-action-ctas)

Where you want to send readers of the articles to your website. Turn each card on or off; the wording changes with each article.

- **Link card** (right after the summary): A branded card with a headline and a button. Add up to 5 pages with their button text, and AI sends the reader to whichever page fits the article best. If you add no pages, it links to your homepage.
- **Map card** (inside the article body): A card that shows your business location. Open your business in Google Maps, tap **Share**, and paste the link to turn it on.
- **Contact card** (end of the article): A card offering a way to reach you. Add a phone number, WhatsApp number, or email address to turn it on. AI picks the channel that suits the article, and your details appear exactly as entered.
- **Promo paragraph** (before the FAQ): A short paragraph that pitches your offer in the article's own words, linked to the best matching page on your site. You can set the page to use when none fits, and attach an optional photo underneath.

![Calls to action settings with the Link card, Map card, Contact card, and Promo paragraph, and a preview of the link card](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-visual-4.c3af8743.png)

#### [YouTube video](#youtube-video)

Embed one relevant video per article, inside the article body. Turn on **Include a video**, then add your channel handle (for example, @ChannelName) to only use your own videos. Leave it empty to search all of YouTube.

![YouTube video settings with the Include a video toggle and a channel handle field](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-visual-5.c197e103.png)

### [Schedule](#schedule)

Control on which days articles are published.

- Select which days of the week articles should be published.
- Toggle individual days on or off based on your content calendar needs.

![Article Schedule settings with a toggle for each day of the week](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/schedule-1.6cba1681.png)

## [Integrations](#integrations)

### [Publishing](#publishing)

Connect your website so finished articles publish automatically. The page is organized into three sections:

- **Website connections:** The **Automatically Publish** toggle, which publishes new articles to your site on auto-pilot.
- **Connect Your Website:** WordPress, WordPress.com, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Ghost, Framer, Lovable, GoHighLevel, Duda, BigCommerce, Snapps, and Next.js Blog. Connect your website once and new articles get published to your blog automatically. Website not supported? Use **Blog Hosting**: add one DNS record and we host your blog on your subdomain.
- **For developers:** No platform match? Publish anywhere with the **API** or **Webhook**.

Each platform card shows **Connect** until it's set up. Click it to open a step-by-step setup wizard; once connected, the card shows **Connected** and you can click **Manage** to change settings. See the **Integrations** guides in the sidebar for per-platform instructions.

![Publishing settings with the Automatically Publish toggle, website platform cards, Blog Hosting, and the API and Webhook developer options](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-publishing.dfe927dc.png)

### [Analytics](#analytics)

Track clicks, impressions, and AI traffic for every article, right from your dashboard.

- **Google Analytics 4:** Track AI-driven traffic and measure content performance.
- **Google Search Console:** See which searches already bring you traffic and let the content engine tailor articles to what works. We only get view-only access.

![Analytics integrations with Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console connect cards](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/integrations-analytics.cb22722f.png)

Once connected, your organic and AI search performance appears in the [Analytics dashboard](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/dashboard/analytics).

## [Socials](#socials)

### [Reddit](#reddit)

Controls how we find Reddit threads worth replying to. See [Reddit AI Agent](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/dashboard/reddit) for how to review and post the opportunities this produces.

- **Reddit Monitored keywords:** The terms we watch for new opportunities. Add your own or pick from the suggestions we generate. Your plan sets how many can be active at once.
- **Subreddits to monitor:** Optional. By default we search all of Reddit. Add subreddits here to narrow the search to the communities that matter to you.
- **Customize your replies:** Tell us how every Reddit reply should be written, for example the tone to use and the phrasing to avoid.

If you run out of keyword slots, the upgrade banner on this page adds more through Reddit+.

### [Quora](#quora)

Controls how we find Quora questions worth answering. See [Quora AI Agent](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/dashboard/quora) for how to review and post the opportunities this produces.

- **Quora Monitored keywords:** The terms we watch for new questions. Add your own, or use the generate button to have keywords suggested from your business profile. Your plan sets how many can be active at once.
- **Customize your replies:** Tell us how every Quora answer should be written, for example the tone to use and the phrasing to avoid.

If you run out of keyword slots, the upgrade banner on this page adds more through Quora+.
