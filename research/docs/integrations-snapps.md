Integrations

# Snapps CMS

Connect BabyLoveGrowth to Snapps with a Site ID and API key, then publish on auto-pilot.

The Snapps integration lets BabyLoveGrowth publish articles directly to your Snapps AI website. Once connected, every newly generated article is published automatically (live or as a draft, your choice).

How articles look on your site

Formatting and mobile-friendliness depend on your website's theme and CSS. We deliver semantic content; your theme controls the styling. [Learn more](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/dashboard/article-quality)

---

## [How to Connect Snapps](#how-to-connect-snapps)

Open **Settings → Publishing**, find **Snapps** under **Website platforms**, and click **Connect** to launch the setup wizard. The wizard walks you through 4 short steps, mirrored below.

Already connected? The Snapps card shows a **Connected** badge. Click **Manage** to update your settings at any time.

### [What You Need](#what-you-need)

- Access to your Snapps dashboard
- Your Snapps API key
- Your Snapps Site ID

### [Open your Snapps dashboard](#open-your-snapps-dashboard)

In the wizard, click **Open Snapps Dashboard** to open Snapps in a new tab, then sign in.

### [Get your API key](#get-your-api-key)

In Snapps, open **Settings** (bottom-left) → **API key**, then copy your key and paste it into the **API Key** field in the wizard.

### [Get your Site ID](#get-your-site-id)

In Snapps, open **My sites** (left sidebar), click the **⋮** menu on the site you want, and choose **Copy Site ID**. Paste it into the **Site ID** field in the wizard.

You can connect one Snapps site per BabyLoveGrowth account.

### [Blog publishing](#blog-publishing)

Set an optional **Author Name** and choose how articles should be published under **Publish Settings**:

- **Publish Immediately**: articles go live as soon as they're generated
- **Draft**: articles are saved as drafts for you to review first

Click **Connect** to finish. You'll see the **Snapps CMS connected!** screen.

## [Publishing & Verifying](#publishing--verifying)

With **Automatically Publish** on (toggle at the top of the Integrations page), new articles publish to Snapps on their own. To verify the connection right away:

1. Go to your **Content Plan**
2. Publish or re-publish any article
3. Check your Snapps site to confirm the article appears

## [Troubleshooting & FAQ](#troubleshooting--faq)

Invalid API key or Site ID?

Re-copy from Snapps **Settings** and **My sites**. Make sure you copied both values correctly.

Publish fails?

Ensure the integration shows **Connected** and the keys are current. Try regenerating your API key if needed.

Connection succeeds but publishing fails?

Verify that your Site ID matches the site you want to publish to, and that the site still exists in Snapps.

Can I set a custom author name?

Yes. Set an author name in Step 4 to display on all published articles.

Will articles go live immediately?

Only if you choose **Publish Immediately**. Choose **Draft** to review in Snapps before going live.

Can I publish to multiple Snapps sites?

You can connect one Snapps site per BabyLoveGrowth account.

What if I regenerate my API key?

The old key stops working immediately. Paste the new key via **Manage** to restore the connection.

Need more help?

Contact support via the chat icon in the bottom-right corner.
