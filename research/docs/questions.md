# FAQ

Common questions about BabyLoveGrowth

## [Getting Started](#getting-started)

I am not an SEO expert?

Don't worry! BabyLoveGrowth is designed to support individuals without any SEO knowledge. We take care of everything from keyword research, clustering, content creation to content optimization. You just need to download the generated articles once per day and publish them on your website.

Do I need to write the articles myself?

No, the whole process is 100% automated and ensures that the content fulfills the user's intent and ranks on Google.

What kind of articles do you produce?

BabyLoveGrowth generates well-researched, citation-backed articles written in your brand's tone and voice, tailored to your target audience. We produce various article types: how-to guides, explainer guides, educational listicles, product roundups, and product comparisons, each matched to the keyword's intent (informational, commercial, or transactional).

## [Content Generation](#content-generation)

How can you generate such high quality content?

Our content generation process leverages the synergy of cutting-edge AI models and human expertise to craft high-quality, SEO-optimized articles. We initiate this process by conducting an in-depth analysis of your webpage to identify topics that resonate with your service or product offerings and target audience. Then, we employ keyword research and clustering techniques to come up with the most relevant and high-potential informational keywords. By examining the existing top-ranking content on Google for these keywords, we develop a comprehensive outline that encompasses all crucial aspects and subtopics. This outline serves as the foundation for generating well-structured articles. Additionally, we ensure the content is optimized for search engines, engaging, and informative, incorporating elements such as relevant images, internal links, FAQ sections, and more.

Are internal links supported?

Yes, internal links are supported. Each article generated by BabyLoveGrowth includes between 3-8 internal links to other relevant articles on your website. Additionally, we also include a "recommended article" section at the end of each article, suggesting further reading materials to your audience.

In which format are the articles generated?

All articles are generated in Markdown format and published directly on your website through one of many CMS integrations (Wordpress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, API,..). You can also download them to your computer.

Why do my articles mention competitors or other products?

This is a critical SEO strategy, not a mistake. It's a special article type 'product listicle comparison'. Google and AI search engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) prioritize comprehensive, unbiased comparisons over promotional content that only mentions one product. If you search "best CRM software" on Google right now, every top result compares multiple products (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.) This is what ranks, what users expect when they search, and what BabyLoveGrowth creates.

When you objectively compare options, you become an authoritative source instead of a salesperson. This builds trust, increases engagement, and matches search intent. People searching for solutions want to see their options, not just read an advertisement.

You can also disable this article type in **Settings → Writing → Mention Competitors When Relevant**.

## [Publishing & Display](#publishing--display)

Why doesn't my article look good on my site?

Formatting, layout, and mobile-friendliness on your site depend on your website's theme and CSS, not BabyLoveGrowth. We deliver clean, semantic content (headings, paragraphs, lists, etc.); your theme controls how it looks (fonts, spacing, responsiveness). If an article doesn't look the way you expect, adjust your theme or add custom CSS for your blog/post templates. See [Article Quality](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/dashboard/article-quality) for more.

Webflow: Blog content not displaying in the Editor?

This is a known issue from the Webflow team. Blog content may not display properly in the Webflow Editor (e.g. visible code, broken layout, Rich Text field issues). You can refer to [this confirmation from Webflow](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EHzmC5ZgHaMCA-uN0KLFzX0lYWROMyXe/view?usp=sharing). Content publishes successfully and will be visible on your live website. See our [Webflow integration guide](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/integrations/webflow) for more details.

## [Ranking & Results](#ranking--results)

Will Google penalize AI written content?

No, Google penalizes low quality content, no matter if it's AI or human written. Their official take on AI content is available here: [Google Search and AI Content](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content).

Can AI content even rank on Google?

Yes, absolutely. If executed correctly. We've proven multiple times that quality AI content can rank well on Google. When AI has a sufficient context window to understand user intent and access to high-quality, credible information about the topic, it produces excellent content. Proper citation prevents hallucinations, and using the best LLM models available ensures quality. We handle all of this for you.

My articles aren't ranking on Google yet. Why?

SEO takes time, and this is completely normal. In the first 3-4 weeks, articles are being discovered and indexed by Google. At 4-8 weeks, articles start appearing in search results, usually on pages 2-5. After 2-3 months, articles begin climbing to page 1 for target keywords. Optimal rankings and consistent traffic typically appear at 3-6 months.

ChatGPT usually picks up articles sooner than Google, often within 1-2 weeks. Check Google Analytics to see ChatGPT traffic. While waiting, keep publishing consistently and don't judge results too early. After 2-3 months, analyze which articles are working and focus on similar topics.

How long does it typically take to see results?

While SEO is a long-term strategy, most clients begin seeing measurable improvements in their search rankings within 3-6 months. However, establishing true topical authority typically takes 6-12 months of consistent content publication and optimization.
