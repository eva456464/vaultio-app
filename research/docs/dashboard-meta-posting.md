# Instagram & Facebook

Learn how to connect your accounts, review, schedule, and post article carousels to Instagram and Facebook.

Instagram & Facebook posting turns each new article into a carousel post for your Facebook Page and Instagram account. Find it in Social Presence under the Instagram & Facebook tab, then review, edit, or let posts go out on your schedule.

![Screenshot of the Instagram & Facebook tab in Social Presence with carousel posts and their posting status](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/meta-1.19b98ec3.png)

## [How It Works](#how-it-works)

### [Connect Facebook & Instagram](#connect-facebook--instagram)

One Facebook sign-in connects both accounts. In Meta's window, select your Facebook Page and the Instagram account linked to it, and keep every permission switched on.

### [Articles become carousels](#articles-become-carousels)

BabyLoveGrowth creates a carousel from each new article, with slides, a caption, and hashtags in your brand voice. The last slide sends people to your website.

### [Review and post](#review-and-post)

Edit the caption and hashtags, then approve the post, post it now, or let it go out at its scheduled time. Your approval setting decides how much happens automatically.

## [Why It Matters](#why-it-matters)

### [More places to find your brand](#more-places-to-find-your-brand)

Every post is another public mention of your brand, giving people, search engines, and AI assistants more places to discover you.

### [Traffic back to your site](#traffic-back-to-your-site)

The last slide of every carousel links to your website, so interested readers can go straight to the full article.

### [No extra writing](#no-extra-writing)

Posts are made from the articles you already publish, so your social presence grows alongside your blog.

## [Before You Connect](#before-you-connect)

- **A Facebook Page:** Posts go to a business Page, not a personal profile. A name and a category are enough to create one.
- **Full control of the Page:** You need full control to let BabyLoveGrowth post. A Page owner can give it in the Page's settings, under Page access.
- **A linked Instagram account:** Instagram must be a Business or Creator account and be linked to that Facebook Page.
- **One Page at a time:** Select one Page and the Instagram linked to it. If you select two Pages, only the first is used.

## [Dashboard Overview](#dashboard-overview)

- **Connection status:** See which Facebook Page and Instagram account you post to, your posting schedule, and any problem that needs fixing.
- **Posts:** Browse your carousels and their status, such as Awaiting approval, Will be posted, Posted, or Failed.
- **Post details:** Preview every slide, see the article it came from, and edit the caption (up to 2,200 characters) and hashtags (up to 15).
- **Post actions:** Approve, Post now, or Delete a post. After posting, open it directly on Facebook or Instagram.
- **Posting settings:** Manage connected accounts, content style, approval mode, and schedule.

## [Posting Settings](#posting-settings)

### [Who approves posts](#who-approves-posts)

- **Automatic:** New posts are approved automatically and posted at the scheduled time on your enabled days.
- **Automatic with review:** Posts wait for your approval, and approved posts go out at their scheduled time. A post you have not approved before its scheduled time waits until you post it manually.
- **Manual:** Nothing is posted automatically. You review, approve, and post every post yourself.

![Review & post settings showing the Automatic, Automatic with review, and Manual options for who approves posts](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/doc-meta-1.cfa4a867.png)

### [Schedule](#schedule)

- **Every:** Pick the days posts go out, from Sun to Sat. The Grow plan allows one posting day a week. Daily posting unlocks every day.
- **at:** Pick the posting time. It is shown in your time zone, with the matching UTC time next to it.
- **to:** Choose Facebook Page, Instagram, or both. At least one account must be selected.

If a schedule change means an already-scheduled post no longer fits, you are asked to confirm. Those posts are not posted automatically and wait until you post them manually.

### [Content](#content)

- **Post categories:** Educational, Philosophical, Myth-busting, and Storytelling. When several are enabled, posts rotate between them.
- **Image style:** Photo realistic or Sketch.
- **Text style:** Bold & clean, Elegant, Playful, or Vintage. This changes only the slide text and brand mark, not the image.
- **Brand name on slides:** Show your domain as a small mark on every slide. The link on the last slide always stays.

Content changes apply to newly generated posts only.

## [Best Practices](#best-practices)

- **Start with review:** Use Automatic with review until you are happy with how posts look, then switch to Automatic.
- **Check every caption:** Verify claims and adjust the tone for your audience before approving.
- **Pick styles that fit your brand:** Choose the image and text styles that match how your brand looks elsewhere.
- **Keep your connection healthy:** Fix connection problems as soon as they appear so scheduled posts are not held back.

## [Frequently Asked Questions](#frequently-asked-questions)

How often are posts created?

The Grow plan includes one post a week on one posting day. The Meta Posting add-on or the Scale plan unlocks daily posting, with a post for every new article. Only workspace admins can subscribe.

Do I need to connect before posts are created?

Your first posts are generated before you connect so you can see what they look like. After that, new posts wait until a Facebook Page is connected. With Meta Posting or the Scale plan, you can connect right away.

Can I post to Facebook without Instagram?

Yes. If no Instagram account is linked to your Page, posts still go to Facebook. You can also turn either account off in Posting settings.

Why is my posting paused?

Posting pauses when Meta ends access to your Page or your profile can no longer post to it. Reconnect from the Instagram & Facebook tab or Posting settings to continue.

What happens to a post that missed its schedule?

It is not posted automatically. It waits in your list until you post it manually.

What happens if I disconnect?

Posting stops right away and stored access to your Page and its Instagram account is deleted. Your posts stay, and you can connect again at any time.
