# Base44

Use Blog Hosting to add a blog to your Base44 app with 1-click DNS setup.

For Base44 apps, use our [Blog Hosting](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/integrations/blog-hosting) feature - just add one DNS record and we'll host your blog at `blog.yoursite.com`.

[### View Blog Hosting Setup Guide](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/integrations/blog-hosting)

When you use Blog Hosting (as above), BabyLoveGrowth controls styling and mobile responsiveness. You can customize logo and colors in hosting settings. If you self-host your blog on your own site instead, appearance depends on your theme and CSS. [Learn more →](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/docs/integrations/blog-hosting#customization-options)
