# Quora AI Agent

Learn how to review, answer, and track Quora opportunities in Social Presence.

Social Presence finds relevant Quora questions where your business can provide a useful answer. Review each opportunity and suggested answer, then post it manually and track your participation.

![Screenshot of Quora opportunities in Social Presence with suggested answers and response controls](https://www.babylovegrowth.ai/_next/static/media/quora-ai-agent.b29510da.png)

## [How It Works](#how-it-works)

### [Agent finds relevant Quora questions for monitored keywords](#agent-finds-relevant-quora-questions-for-monitored-keywords)

BabyLoveGrowth searches Quora using your active monitored keywords and keeps opportunities that match your business and language.

### [Review and answer](#review-and-answer)

Open the full question, read the existing answers, and generate a suggested answer. Edit the draft so it is accurate, specific, and useful before posting it on Quora.

### [Track your participation](#track-your-participation)

Mark the opportunity as commented in BabyLoveGrowth after posting. Consistent, helpful answers build your public presence and authority over time.

## [Why It Matters](#why-it-matters)

### [Reach people looking for help](#reach-people-looking-for-help)

Quora questions often describe a specific problem or ask for a recommendation, giving you a natural opportunity to share relevant expertise.

### [Build search visibility](#build-search-visibility)

Public Quora pages can appear in Google results, putting a useful answer in front of more people searching for the same topic.

### [Support brand and AI visibility](#support-brand-and-ai-visibility)

Helpful public answers create another place where people, search engines, and AI assistants can discover your expertise and brand.

## [Dashboard Overview](#dashboard-overview)

- **Manage Keywords:** Add, enable, or disable the searches used to find Quora opportunities.
- **To comment and Commented:** Separate open opportunities from questions you have already answered.
- **Opportunity details:** Review the question and monitored keyword before answering.
- **Suggested Answers:** Generate a draft when needed, edit it, then use Copy & open or Mark as commented.

## [Best Practices](#best-practices)

- **Read the full discussion:** Review the question and existing answers before posting.
- **Lead with a direct answer:** Help the reader before mentioning your brand.
- **Edit every draft:** Verify its claims and adapt its tone to the conversation.
- **Be specific:** Add practical steps or examples that demonstrate expertise.
- **Stay relevant:** Skip questions outside your services, language, or target market.

## [Frequently Asked Questions](#frequently-asked-questions)

Are all matching Quora questions shown?

No. Questions must pass a relevance check based on your business and language before they appear as opportunities.

Should I post the generated answer unchanged?

No. Treat it as a draft, verify every claim, and adapt it to the original question before posting.

Does answering guarantee Google rankings or AI mentions?

No. A useful public answer makes your expertise more discoverable, but rankings and mentions are not guaranteed.

Do I need to find questions manually?

No. The agent finds opportunities from your monitored keywords so you can focus on reviewing and answering them.
