## [What Is Backlink?](#what-is-backlink)

A **backlink** is a link from another website that points to your website. It serves as a signal to search engines that your content is credible and valuable.

**Example:** If an authoritative industry blog links to your article, it suggests to Google that your page should be considered more valuable or trustworthy than similar pages without a backlink.

**Why it matters:**

- Helps grow your domain authority (DR)
- Improves your chances of ranking higher on Google and in AI answers (such as ChatGPT, Perplexity)
- Can earn you qualified referral traffic
- Builds brand credibility

## [Dashboard Overview](#dashboard-overview)

- **Domain Rating:** Your current DR, powered by Ahrefs. The chart shows the last few months as a solid line and a dotted projection of where your DR is heading, with a badge for the projected point increase. DR gains follow diminishing returns, so newer sites are projected to climb faster than established ones.
- **How your DR grows:** Shows where you sit on the authority scale. You are matched with sites of similar authority, because links between comparable sites look the most natural to search engines. As your DR grows, you start matching with higher-authority sites.
- **Backlinks Received:** Total number of backlinks you have received, with the **estimated value** of what those links would cost to buy on a marketplace.
- **Backlinks Given:** Total number of backlinks you have given to other sites in the network. Giving links is how you earn credits.
- **Available Credits:** Your current credit balance for receiving backlinks. It can be negative for a while, which is expected (see [Why are my credits negative?](#negative-credits)).

## [How the Credit System Works](#how-the-credit-system-works)

A transparent credit model keeps exchanges fair so high-authority domains receive proportional value.

- **Starting Balance:** Every new account begins with 100 credits to start receiving backlinks immediately.
- **Earn Credits:** When you give a backlink (happens automatically), you earn credits equal to your Domain Rating, up to 25 credits per backlink.
- **Spend Credits:** When you receive a backlink (happens automatically), you spend credits based on the giving website's Domain Rating.
- **Self-Balancing:** A link from a higher-authority site is worth more credits than giving one earns, so your balance can dip below zero for a while. It rebalances automatically as your site keeps publishing.
- **No Monthly Cap:** Receive as many backlinks as your credits allow.
- **Optional Top-Up:** Purchase additional credits if you want faster growth. With a backlink subscription, all your backlinks come from sites with DR 20 or higher. Subscriptions are entirely optional.

## [How Backlinks Are Placed](#how-backlinks-are-placed)

- Links are naturally integrated into daily articles published on participating websites within your niche.
- Each link is contextually placed where it adds genuine value for readers.
- All links are dofollow, passing full SEO value.
- Your articles must be live and in your sitemap for placement and verification.

## [Requirements for Approval](#requirements-for-approval)

To participate in the backlink exchange, your site must meet these criteria:

- **Publish regularly:** Fresh, live articles create placement opportunities. Not required if you have only a backlink subscription and not a main subscription.
- **Sitemap coverage:** Published articles must be in your sitemap so the network can discover and verify them.
- **Accessible pages:** No hard paywalls or blocks on target URLs.
- **Relevant niche:** Stay in your niche to receive contextual, high-value links.
- **Credits available:** While your balance is negative, new incoming links pause briefly until your site gives links back. This usually takes a few days to a week, or you can top up to resume right away.

## [Why Some Sites Are Not Approved](#why-some-sites-are-not-approved)

Sites may be rejected or paused for the following reasons:

- Adult, illegal, or gray hat content.
- Very new sites typically need 15 to 20 days before first placements as trust builds.

## [Timing and What to Expect](#timing-and-what-to-expect)

- **Ongoing:** No monthly limit. Links flow continuously as long as you have credits and relevant targets.

## [Frequently Asked Questions](#frequently-asked-questions)

Do I need a backlink subscription?

No. Subscriptions are optional. Use them only if you want more credits faster. Earning credits by giving links works fine on its own.

Why have I not received any links yet?

New or newly approved sites typically take 15 to 20 days. Ensure your articles are published, included in your sitemap, and publicly accessible.

Why was my site not approved?

Common reasons include missing sitemap, blocked pages, off-policy content. Fix these issues and reapply.

Is there a monthly limit on backlinks?

No. You can receive as many backlinks as your credits and relevance allow.

Why are my credits negative?

A negative balance is normal and nothing is broken. It means you've received more backlink value than you've given so far, which is good for you.

**Why it happens**

Receiving a backlink spends credits based on the giving site's Domain Rating, while giving a backlink earns credits based on yours. A link from a site with a higher Domain Rating than yours costs more than one of your own links earns, so after a few strong incoming links your balance can dip below zero. While it's negative, new incoming backlinks pause.

**What you can do**

You have two options:

1. **Wait (free).** Keep publishing. The system automatically places backlinks to other sites in your upcoming articles, and each one earns you credits. Your balance usually turns positive again within a few days to a week, and incoming backlinks resume on their own.
2. **Get a backlink subscription.** It adds bonus credits to your account right away, so you start receiving backlinks again immediately, all from sites with DR 20 or higher.

Do I lose credits when I give links?

No. Giving links earns credits. Receiving links spends credits. If your balance goes negative, incoming links pause briefly until it rebalances.

Do these links help with AI visibility?

Yes. Quality backlinks improve authority signals that both search engines and AI systems consider when citing sources.

Are there limited seats for this subscription?

Yes. There are 20 total slots across the platform. The frontend shows remaining slots (e.g., "5 slots left"). When all slots are taken, new purchases are blocked until a slot becomes available.

What is a backlink subscription?

An optional monthly add-on that adds bonus backlink credits (100, 300, or 600 credits/month) to your account. While it is active, all backlinks your website receives come from sites with DR 20 or higher. Credits roll over month to month. Anyone can buy it, and it also lets you receive backlinks without publishing any articles.
